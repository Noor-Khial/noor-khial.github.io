Noor is an ML/AI Engineer at Malomatia, Qatar, working on AI systems for customs and logistics under the direction of Khadeja Al-Mahmoud. In parallel, she has been a researcher at Qatar University since 2023, in the group of Prof. Amr Mohamed. She earned her MSc in Computing (2025, Research Distinguished Award) on a Graduate Research Assistantship, with a thesis on path planning for UAV missions under uncertainty, and her BSc in Computer Engineering (2022, Amiri Diwan Scholarship) from Qatar University, where her capstone built the onboard architecture of QUbeSat I, the university's first CubeSat, led by Prof. Tamer Khattab.

Noor is interested in how learning systems can sustain performance during deployment under partially known conditions. Her work focuses on settings where the user behavior changes in ways that aren't known in advance, with the goal of designing learning algorithms that adapt to these changes in real time. In practice, she builds AI models from offline data and adapts them to the unpredictable traffic of live systems.
