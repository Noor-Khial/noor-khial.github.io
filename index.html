Noor is an ML/AI Engineer at Malomatia, Qatar, where she has worked on projects related to customs and logistics for the past year, under the direction of Khadeja Al-Mahmoud. Parallel to this, she has been a researcher at Qatar University under several funded projects since 2023, within the group of Prof. Amr Mohamed (group). Her work during MSc studies was recognized by the Research Distinguished Award and published in top-ranked AI journals. Prior to this, during her BSc capstone, she worked on the architecture of QUbeSat I, the first CubeSat launched under Qatar University, led by Prof. Tamer Khattab.

Noor is interested in how learning systems can sustain performance during deployment under partially known conditions. Her work focuses on settings where the user behavior changes in ways that aren't known in advance, with the goal of designing learning algorithms that adapt to these changes in real time. In practice, she builds AI models from offline data and adapts them to the unpredictable traffic of live systems.
